NIF extraction schema attribution

nif_schema.xml is a reduced parsing-metadata adaptation of niftools/nifxml,
https://github.com/niftools/nifxml/blob/develop/nif.xml, retrieved 2026-10-30.
The adaptation omits prose, default values and non-reading attributes; version
conditions and field definitions are retained. Upstream license: GPL-3.0.
See NIFXML-LICENSE.txt. This metadata and the independent conversion tools are
separate build-time tools; the browser game reads only exported asset data.
The original Empire Earth II game assets remain the property of their authors.
